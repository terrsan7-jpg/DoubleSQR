Engineering

Every line, defensible.

Behind every render is a coordinated set of drawings — architectural, structural, MEP. Each line answers a code requirement, a load case, or a constructability question. Watch one assemble.

Loads resolved.

Gravity loads from the roof and slabs are gathered at each floor and routed down the columns into reinforced footings. Every member is sized to the design code with appropriate factors of safety — and documented in the structural package handed to the contractor.
